Common questions

Can I switch plans later?

Yes - upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account. Changes apply to your next billing cycle.

Does Auto mode cost extra?

Auto mode (unattended drafting of applications and outreach) is included on Pro and above for every role. It's not available on free tiers.

Is there a free trial for paid plans?

Every paid plan starts with a 14-day trial - no card required to start, cancel anytime before it ends.
